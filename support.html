How do I connect a Google calendar?

Open the Alcove settings and add an account. Choose Google and sign in. Then choose the calendars to show.

How do I connect an iCloud calendar?

iCloud needs an app-specific password. Your Apple Account password does not work. To make one, sign in at account.apple.com, open Sign-In and Security, and choose App-Specific Passwords. Then enter your Apple ID and the new password in Alcove.

Why do I not see an event?

Check that the calendar is turned on in the Alcove settings. Then check that the device is online. If the event is still missing, remove the account and add it again.

How do I add or change an event?

Use the add button on the screen. Choose the calendar for the new event. To change an event, open it from the calendar. The change goes to your calendar service.

The weather is wrong or missing.

Alcove needs a location to show the weather. Check the location in the Alcove settings and allow location access if you use your current location. The weather comes from Open-Meteo.

How do I dim the screen at night?

Open the Alcove settings. Turn on the dim mode, or set the quiet hours for the times when you sleep.

How do I remove my accounts and my data?

Remove each account in the Alcove settings. Then delete the app. Alcove has no account of its own and keeps no copy of your calendar data. For more information, read the Privacy Policy.
